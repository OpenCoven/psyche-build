# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

OpenCoven contributors must interact with respect, patience, and intellectual
honesty. We welcome disagreement about architecture and product direction when
it stays focused on evidence and the work.

Expected behavior includes:

- assuming good faith while still reviewing security and correctness rigorously;
- giving actionable feedback without insults, threats, or humiliation;
- respecting names, boundaries, privacy, and differing levels of experience;
- disclosing conflicts of interest and material uncertainty;
- protecting credentials, private project data, and non-public reports; and
- correcting mistakes openly when evidence changes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, sexualized
attention, doxxing, intimidation, sustained disruption, retaliation, or using
private/security information to pressure another contributor.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
OpenCoven community spaces, and private project communication connected to this
repository.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report urgent threats through the relevant platform safety channel. Report other
conduct concerns privately to an OpenCoven maintainer or moderator. Do not post
names, screenshots, private messages, or sensitive details in a public issue.

If no private contact path is available, open a minimal issue requesting private
maintainer contact without identifying anyone or describing the incident.

Maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation,
temporarily suspend access, or permanently exclude a participant. Enforcement
should be proportionate, documented privately, free of retaliation, and handled
by a maintainer without a conflict of interest whenever possible.

Good-faith reports and appeals must not be punished. A person affected by an
enforcement decision may request review by a different available maintainer.
